Privacy Policy

App Name: DELICIOUS CAKE CORNER — Bakery POS & Billing

Package Name: com.deliciouscakecorner.pos

Last Updated: November 2026

At DELICIOUS CAKE CORNER ("we", "our", or "us"), operating in Mansuka, Ghatal, West Bengal, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our bakery customers, staff, and store managers. This Privacy Policy describes how our Point of Sale (POS) & Billing application collects, uses, and safeguards information.

1. Information We Collect

Our application operates primarily offline as a local retail billing system. We collect only the minimum information necessary to print invoices, send receipts, and manage cake advance orders:

  • Customer Contact Details: Customer name and phone number provided voluntarily at billing for receipt delivery via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Order & Billing Details: Items purchased, customized cake messages (e.g., "Happy Birthday"), delivery address, delivery date, and payment status (Cash, UPI, Card).
  • Store & Hardware Configurations: Thermal printer settings, counter profiles, and terminal preferences stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Generating and printing paper or digital tax receipts and delivery slips.
  • Delivering order confirmations, delivery alerts, and birthday reminders via SMS or WhatsApp upon customer request.
  • Tracking daily sales totals, inventory deduction, and customer loyalty reward points.

3. Data Storage & Security

All billing records and order data are stored securely on the merchant's local device storage (Browser LocalStorage / IndexedDB). If enabled by the store administrator, data may be backed up to the merchant's private Google Sheets or secured Google Cloud Firestore database. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer data to third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

The application interacts with standard external services solely for operational POS functionality:

  • UPI Payment Rails: Generates standard NPCI Bharat UPI QR codes (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) for direct merchant-to-customer contactless settlements.
  • Google Maps & OpenStreetMap: Used strictly for route calculation and delivery distance estimations for home delivery cake orders in Ghatal & Mansuka.
  • SMS Gateways (Fast2SMS / Twilio / MSG91): Used exclusively to deliver customer receipt messages if configured.

5. Device Permissions & Camera / Location

The app may request optional device permissions only when actively invoked by the user:

  • Camera / QR Scanner: Optional, used solely to scan customer membership cards or digital gift coupons.
  • Bluetooth / USB: Optional, used solely to transmit ESC/POS commands to ESC/POS thermal receipt printers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is a commercial bakery Point of Sale tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
